Services Offered

What We
Cover

From routine drain maintenance to full hydro jetting, backflow testing to heating and gas systems — complete commercial plumbing for your portfolio.

Standard Plumbing
Repairs, fixture installation, pipe maintenance, and all routine plumbing for commercial and multi-residential properties. Mon–Fri at $140/hr.
Hydro Jetting
High-pressure water jetting to clear blocked drains, grease buildup, and stubborn blockages throughout your building's drain systems. Equipment: $295.
Camera Inspection
Advanced camera diagnostics to locate blockages, cracks, and root intrusions before they escalate into costly emergencies. Equipment: $450.
Drain Cleaning
Professional drain cleaning for floor drains, stack lines, and common-area plumbing. Hand auger ($20) and full drain cleaning equipment ($245).
Backflow Testing
Certified backflow prevention device testing at $115/unit. Volume pricing for 10+ units. Jurisdiction and city fees may apply separately.
Heating & Gas
Licensed heating and gas services for boilers and mechanical systems. Regular rate $160/hr · After-hours $240/hr. Always safe, always compliant.
